Transmission Control Protocol / Internet Protocol (TCP/IP)

Definition

TCP/IP is a set of communication protocols used to connect devices over the internet.
It governs how data is transmitted, addressed, received, and reconstructed.

Role in Internet Technology

TCP/IP ensures reliable data transmission by breaking data into packets, sending them
across networks, and reassembling them at the destination. It allows communication between
different devices regardless of their hardware or software differences.
